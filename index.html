The equipment behind the quality

We work smarter because we know the craft.

2021 Mecalac 8MCR

Our versatile machine takes on excavation, skid-steer-style material handling, lifting and loading. We dig footings, move rock and earth, and can place gravel into house slabs from outside the forms, subject to safe machine capacity and site conditions.

Riding Trowels & Pans

We use riding power trowels on many slabs and believe in pan finishing slab-on-grade work before switching to trowel blades when conditions and specifications allow. Proper timing, equipment and technique matter.

Straight, Clean Decorative Joints

After broom finishing, we make early-entry Soff-Cut saw cuts at the appropriate time. The next day, we route and detail selected joints to create clean, straight lines with rounded, hand-tooled-looking edges.

Who we are

Still here. Still pouring. Still grateful.

We're concrete finishers first—good old country boys who learned our trade by showing up and doing the work. Years in commercial and industrial concrete taught us demanding standards, specialized equipment and how to handle bigger pours.

We also learned some hard lessons. We stepped into a world where capital and corporate scale matter as much as craftsmanship, and we took some hits. Some folks around here saw it happen. We're not hiding from it.

Today we're bringing that experience home, focusing on residential foundations, larger slabs and small-business concrete projects. We're grateful to be here, grateful to be pouring and determined to earn trust one job at a time.

We show up. We do the work. We take pride in our craft. And you're welcome to watch.
