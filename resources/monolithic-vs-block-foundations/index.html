Monolithic Slab vs. Block Foundation: What Should You Consider?

Both approaches can work well. The correct choice depends on the building design, soil, elevation, and site conditions.

Monolithic foundations

Where approved, a monolithic placement can combine thickened footings and slab in a coordinated pour, reducing separate stages.

CMU or block foundations

Block foundations can provide elevation flexibility and crawlspace options, depending on the design.

Inspections and trades

Plumbing, reinforcement, termite protection, moisture control, and inspections still have to happen in the right order.

Do not choose on price alone

Structural plans, bearing conditions, drainage, and local code requirements should lead the decision. A licensed design professional may be needed.
