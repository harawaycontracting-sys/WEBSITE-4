Metal Building Slab Checklist: What Your Concrete Contractor Needs

Building a shop, pole barn, or PEMB? Get these details together before you ask for a slab price.

Approved drawings and building footprint

Send the latest foundation and anchor bolt plans, including column locations and all dimensions. Early sketches are useful for budgeting but not for setting anchors.

Intended use and loading

Vehicle lifts, forklifts, heavy equipment, and storage racks can require different design assumptions. Tell us about them early.

Concrete and subbase requirements

Identify thickness, specified compressive strength, reinforcement, vapor retarder, aggregate, and any floor finish or flatness requirements.

Site conditions and access

Photos, elevation changes, drainage, existing fill, and truck access help us plan the placement.

Coordination with the erector

Give us the building supplier and installer contact information so anchor locations, templates, elevations, and schedule are coordinated.
