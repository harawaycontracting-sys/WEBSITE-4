What a well-built project starts with

We work with homeowners and builders on porches, patios, shop pads, equipment pads, and additions. The goal is a finished surface that connects properly to the property and drains where it should.

How we approach the work

  1. Review dimensions, use, grade, access, and tie-ins.
  2. Discuss removal, base preparation, reinforcement, and surface finish.
  3. Coordinate stairs, thresholds, utilities, and drainage as needed.
  4. Place, finish, joint, and cure the slab to the agreed scope.
Bring us the real details.

Plans, dimensions, photographs, site address, intended use, and target schedule help us prepare a more useful proposal. Unusual loads, structural foundations, and site drainage may require approved engineering or permits.

Frequently asked questions

Can you extend an existing patio?

Often yes. We will review elevation, drainage, and the existing slab condition before recommending a connection detail.

Do you pour shop and equipment pads?

Yes. We can quote them with the intended loads and any engineering or anchorage requirements.
