What a well-built project starts with

For qualifying straightforward projects, our advertised starting price is $5.25 per square foot. Send the drawings, jobsite location, and installer information. We would rather coordinate the details with your metal installer than leave you stuck between trades.

How we approach the work

  1. Confirm the building footprint, slab elevation, thickness, and edge details.
  2. Price any gravel fill, grading, vapor retarder, and special reinforcement.
  3. Coordinate post locations, door openings, and slab edges with the installer.
  4. Plan concrete placement, finish, sawcut joints, and specified curing treatment.
  5. Discuss optional interior wash drains where drainage design and site conditions allow.
Bring us the real details.

Plans, dimensions, photographs, site address, intended use, and target schedule help us prepare a more useful proposal. Unusual loads, structural foundations, and site drainage may require approved engineering or permits.

Frequently asked questions

Does $5.25 per square foot include everything?

No. It is an advertised starting rate for qualifying projects, not a binding quote. Excavation, access, engineered foundations, concrete specifications, and minimum job size can change pricing.

Can you install a wash drain?

Optional wash drains may start around $250 for simple qualifying installations; drain body, discharge routing, plumbing, permits, and site conditions must be reviewed.
